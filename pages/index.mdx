## How a league runs

1. **Install and activate** — invite the bot, get the guild activated, run `/sync-users`.
2. **Configure once** — `/config` sets regions, roster size, match days, and scheduling windows.
3. **Teams form** — captains manage their own rosters and availability.
4. **Schedules publish weekly** — generated from availability and elo bounds, with captain DMs and reminders.
5. **Matches record themselves** — on tracked servers results and stats are captured automatically; otherwise `/report-match` takes seconds.
6. **Profiles update** — ratings recompute and [heir.gg](https://heir.gg) profiles publish on their own.

## Conventions in these docs

- Commands are written as `/command subcommand`.
- Anything marked **(Organizer)** requires the Organizer role or a server admin.
- Anything marked **(Captain)** is limited to a team's captain.
- IDs shown in examples are Discord snowflakes.
