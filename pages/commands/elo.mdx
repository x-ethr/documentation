# `/elo`

Organizer-style ELO tools. Access is controlled per-server via **Server Settings →
Integrations** (hidden from members by default).

## `/elo match`

Record a match manually and update both teams' ELO and records.

- `winner` `Role`
- `loser` `Role`
- `winning_score` `Number`
- `losing_score` `Number`

This is the organizer-side alternative to `/report-match`.

Scores must be non-negative whole numbers and the winning score must be greater than the
losing score — if the reported winner lost, swap the teams. See
[Mathematics](/platform/ratings) for how ratings are calculated.

If the two teams have a scheduled fixture on the active published schedule, the result is
linked to it automatically and the schedule embed updates (see
[`/schedule`](/commands/schedule#published-schedules)). You can also
record results straight from the published schedule with its **Report result** button.

## `/elo override`

Set a team's ELO directly.

- `team` `Role`
- `elo` `Number`
