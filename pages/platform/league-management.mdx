# League Management

Live leagues are managed from the web at **your league page → ⋮ → Manage
league**, or directly at `heir.gg/<server-id>/manage`.

## Who can do what

Heir resolves permissions **live from Discord** every time — nothing is stored,
so role changes take effect within minutes.

| Rung | Can |
|---|---|
| Anyone | View standings, players, matches, tier lists |
| Signed in | Everything above, plus their own dashboard, settings, and linked accounts |
| Player | Appears in leagues; joins teams |
| **Organizer** (role) | Manage league settings from the web; all organizer bot commands |
| **Server owner** | Everything an Organizer can, plus setup, cancel, and **deleting the league** |

## League settings

The management page covers what `/config` covers, from the browser: regions,
match nights, series format, roster size, the channels Heir posts to, the ref
role, and stat approval. Changes are applied **by the bot itself** — the page
waits for Heir's confirmation before reporting a save, and every change is
recorded in the league's audit log.

<Callout type="info">
The <strong>game</strong> is fixed at setup and cannot be changed — switching
games would orphan every linked account, stat, and rating.
</Callout>

## Deleting a league

Owner only, from the league page's **⋮ menu**. Deletion asks you to type the
league's exact name, then permanently removes:

- every team, match, stat, rating, and schedule
- the league's audit history and registered game servers
- the channels and roles Heir created
- Heir itself — the bot leaves your server

<Callout type="warning">
There is no undo. Heir re-verifies both your ownership and the typed name
before deleting anything.
</Callout>

## Transferring your server

If you transfer Discord server ownership, Heir follows automatically: the new
owner gains owner-level access (management, deletion) within a few minutes,
and the previous owner keeps only whatever roles they still hold. There is
nothing to reconfigure.
