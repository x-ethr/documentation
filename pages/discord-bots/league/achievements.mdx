# Achievements

Achievements are earned by playing in the league and are shown on your
[`/player-card`](/discord-bots/league/commands/player-card).

## How they work

- **Achievements are permanent.** Once you earn one, it's yours forever — even if the
  streak ends, you leave the team, or your winrate recovers.
- They're checked every time a player card is viewed; anything you newly qualify for is
  awarded on the spot.
- Match-based achievements count your **current team's matches since you joined it** —
  joining a stacked roster doesn't inherit its history. Leave the team and the counters
  start fresh with your next team, but everything already earned stays.
- Tiers stack: reaching 🦗 Team Hopper doesn't take away 🎖️ Veteran.

<Callout type={"info"}>
    Streak-based achievements like 🔥 On Fire are only awarded while the condition is
    live — someone has to view your card during the streak to lock it in. Claim your
    moment.
</Callout>

## The full list

### Team journey

| Achievement | Earned by |
|---|---|
| 👑 Captain | Being captain of your team |
| 🎖️ Veteran | Having played on 3+ different teams |
| 🦗 Team Hopper | Having played on 5+ different teams |
| 🛡️ Loyal | 90+ days on your current team |
| 💍 Ride or Die | A full year on your current team |

### Winning (and losing)

| Achievement | Earned by |
|---|---|
| 🩸 First Blood | Winning a match with your current team |
| 🔥 On Fire | A 3+ match win streak |
| 🥶 Ice Cold | A 3+ match losing streak |
| 🎢 Rollercoaster | 6 matches in a row strictly alternating win/loss |
| 💎 Undefeated | 5+ matches with your current team, zero losses |
| ⚔️ Grinder | 10+ matches played with your current team |
| 🪙 Perfectly Balanced | Exactly 50% winrate over 10+ matches |
| 📉 Rebuilding Era | 25% winrate or worse over 10+ matches |

### Giant killing

| Achievement | Earned by |
|---|---|
| 🗡️ Giant Slayer | Beating a team rated 300+ above yours |
| 🐲 Dragon Slayer | 3+ such upsets |

### Scoreline specials

| Achievement | Earned by |
|---|---|
| 🧹 Clean Sweep | 3+ shutout wins (opponent won zero rounds) |
| 😰 Clutch Merchant | 3+ wins decided by a single round |
| 💔 Heartbreak Kid | 3+ losses decided by a single round |

<Callout type={"info"}>
    Upsets are measured by the ratings **before** the match — see
    [Mathematics](/discord-bots/league/mathematics) for how ratings are calculated.
</Callout>
