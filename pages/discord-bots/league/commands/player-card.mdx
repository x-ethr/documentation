# `/player-card`

View a player's card.

## Options

- `player` `User` optional

If omitted, the bot shows your own card.

## Card Data

The player card includes:

- region
- current team
- past teams
- earned badges

## Badges

Badges are computed from the player's history and their current team's matches since
they joined it:

| Badge | Earned by |
|---|---|
| 👑 Captain | Currently captain of your team |
| 🩸 First Blood | Winning a match with your current team |
| 🔥 On Fire | A current 3+ match win streak |
| 🗡️ Giant Slayer | Beating a team rated 300+ above yours |
| 💎 Undefeated | 5+ matches with your current team, zero losses |
| ⚔️ Grinder | 10+ matches played with your current team |
| 🎖️ Veteran | Having played on 3+ different teams |
| 🛡️ Loyal | 90+ days on your current team |
