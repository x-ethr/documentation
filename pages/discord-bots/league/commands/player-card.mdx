# `/player-card`

View a player's card.

## Options

- `player` `User` optional

If omitted, the bot shows your own card.

## Card Data

The player card includes:

- region
- current team
- past teams
- earned achievements

## Achievements

**Achievements are permanent — once earned, they're yours forever**, even if you change
teams or the streak ends. They're checked (and any new ones awarded) every time your
card is viewed.

See the full list of all 18 and how each is earned on the
[Achievements](/discord-bots/league/achievements) page.
