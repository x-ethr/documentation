# `/match-history`

View a team's recent matches — date, result, score, opponent, and the rating change
each match produced.

## Options

- `team` `Role` optional — defaults to your own team
- `page` `Integer` optional — 10 matches per page, newest first

## Notes

- Replies are posted publicly in the channel.
- Voided matches are excluded.
- The rating change shown is the exact recorded change for that team (the loser's drop
  can be smaller than the winner's gain when the rating floor applies).
- Page 1 also shows the page's W-L record and the team's current rating.
