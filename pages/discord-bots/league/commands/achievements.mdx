# `/achievements`

Posts the **Achievement Leaderboard** — the top 10 players with the most
achievements — publicly in the channel. Each entry shows the player, their count,
and the emoji of every achievement they've earned.

Ranked by total achievements earned (see the full list on the
[Achievements](/discord-bots/league/achievements) page). Achievements are awarded
when player cards are viewed, so the leaderboard reflects what's been earned and
recorded so far.
