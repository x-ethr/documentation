# `/roster`

Captain-facing roster management plus roster viewing.

## `/roster view`

View a team's roster: elo, standing, record, region, players, and past names. If the
team has a role icon (`/edit-team icon:`), it appears as the embed's thumbnail.

- `team` `Role`

## `/roster add`

Add a player to your team.

- `player` `User`

Requirements enforced by the bot:

- you must be a captain
- the target must not already be on a team
- the target must have the `Free Agent` role
- roster size cannot exceed the guild roster limit
- cooldown rules apply

## `/roster kick`

Kick a player from your team.

- `player` `User`

## `/roster set-captain`

Transfer captaincy to another player on your team.

- `player` `User`
