---
title: "Installation"
---

The fastest way to start a league is the guided setup at
[heir.gg/start](https://heir.gg/start) — it configures everything in about a
minute and activates your league immediately. No approval step, no waiting.

Setup asks one question up front: **do you want a Discord server, or not?**

- **With Discord** — teams are Discord roles, captains can use slash commands, and
  the bot runs matchmaking and stat tracking in your server.
- **Web only** — a league that lives entirely on [heir.gg](https://heir.gg), with
  no Discord server and no bot required.

You can pick what the league runs — **League**, **Tournaments**, or both — either
way, and change it later in Settings.

## With Discord

<Steps>
### Sign in

Go to [heir.gg/start](https://heir.gg/start) and sign in with Discord. Heir
only asks for your identity — never your server list, never your email.

### Describe your league

Pick your **game**, regions, match nights, series format, and roster size.
Everything except the game can be changed later.

<Callout type="info">
A league runs exactly <strong>one game</strong>, and the choice locks when
setup completes. Running two games means running two leagues — each gets its
own server.
</Callout>

### Add Heir to your server

Click **Add to Discord**. Discord shows you the servers you manage; pick the
one this league lives in. You'll come straight back to the wizard — your
answers are saved, even if you close the tab.

On joining, Heir creates its channels (read-me, matchmaking, scores,
leaderboard, logs) and the three league roles: **Organizer**, **Captain**, and
**Free Agent**.

### Pass the checks

The wizard verifies your server is actually ready: Heir's permissions, its
role position, the league roles, and slash-command access. Anything that needs
fixing is shown with exactly what to do about it — fix it and re-check.

### Pick a plan and finish

Choose Free (everything a league needs, no card) and finish. Your league
activates **immediately** — activation never waits on billing.
</Steps>

## Web only

<Steps>
### Sign in

Sign in with Discord at [heir.gg/start](https://heir.gg/start) — your Discord
account is still your Heir account, you just won't connect a server.

### Name your league and pick what it runs

Give the league a name and choose **League**, **Tournaments**, or both.

### Pick a plan and finish

Choose a plan and finish. The league is created on the spot — no bot to invite,
no permission checks — and you land straight in it as the owner. Teams are
records you create from the web, and everything is managed at
`heir.gg/<league>/manage`.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
A web-only league can't run in-game stat tracking (that needs the bot on a game
server), but everything else — teams, ratings, schedules, tournaments, and public
profiles — works the same.
</Callout>

## After setup

1. Players grab the **Free Agent** role from `#get-roles` (Discord leagues), or
   are added to teams directly on the web.
2. Captains create teams — `/team create` in Discord, or the team tools on the web.
3. Players link their game account with `/identity link`, or from their
   [account settings](https://heir.gg/settings) on the web.

## Changed your mind?

- **Start over** (bottom of the wizard) discards your draft and begins again.
- **Cancel** on a Discord league removes everything: Heir deletes the channels
  and roles it created, removes the league's records, and leaves your server.
  Deleting a web-only league removes its records. Cancelling before the install
  just clears the draft.

<Callout type="warning">
For Discord leagues, only the <strong>server owner</strong> can run setup or
cancel it. Once the league is live, day-to-day management opens up to Organizers
too — see <a href="/platform/league-management">League Management</a>.
</Callout>
