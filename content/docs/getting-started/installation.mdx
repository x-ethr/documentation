---
title: "Installation"
---

The fastest way to start a league is the guided setup at
[heir.gg/start](https://heir.gg/start) — it configures everything in about a
minute and activates your league immediately. No approval step, no waiting.

<Steps>
### Sign in

Go to [heir.gg/start](https://heir.gg/start) and sign in with Discord. Heir
only asks for your identity — never your server list, never your email.

### Describe your league

Pick your **game**, regions, match nights, series format, and roster size.
Everything except the game can be changed later.

<Callout type="info">
A league runs exactly <strong>one game</strong>, and the choice locks when
setup completes. Running two games means running two leagues — each gets its
own server.
</Callout>

### Add Heir to your server

Click **Add to Discord**. Discord shows you the servers you manage; pick the
one this league lives in. You'll come straight back to the wizard — your
answers are saved, even if you close the tab.

On joining, Heir creates its channels (read-me, matchmaking, scores,
leaderboard, logs) and the three league roles: **Organizer**, **Captain**, and
**Free Agent**.

### Pass the checks

The wizard verifies your server is actually ready: Heir's permissions, its
role position, the league roles, and slash-command access. Anything that needs
fixing is shown with exactly what to do about it — fix it and re-check.

### Pick a plan and finish

Choose Free (everything a league needs, no card) and finish. Your league
activates **immediately** — activation never waits on billing.
</Steps>

## After setup

1. Players grab the **Free Agent** role from `#get-roles` (Heir handles the
   button itself).
2. Captains create teams with `/team create`.
3. Players link their game account with `/identity link` — or from their
   [account settings](https://heir.gg/settings) on the web.

## Changed your mind?

- **Start over** (bottom of the wizard) discards your draft and begins again.
- **Cancel** removes everything: Heir deletes the channels and roles it
  created, removes the league's records, and leaves your server. Cancelling
  before the install just clears the draft.

<Callout type="warning">
Only the <strong>server owner</strong> can run setup or cancel it. Once the
league is live, day-to-day management opens up to Organizers too — see
<a href="/platform/league-management">League Management</a>.
</Callout>
