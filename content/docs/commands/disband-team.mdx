---
title: "`/disband-team`"
---

Disband your current team.

## Confirmation

The command doesn't act immediately: it replies with an ephemeral summary of
what disbanding means and a red **confirm** button. Nothing happens until you
click it — dismissing the message cancels.

## What It Does

- Marks the team as disbanded in the database
- Removes the captain pointer
- Removes all team members from the roster
- Gives affected members the `Free Agent` role
- Archives the team role instead of deleting it
- Updates the leaderboard

The archived role can be used later with `/reform-team`.
