---
title: "`/achievements`"
---

Posts the **Achievement Leaderboard** — the top 10 players with the most
achievements — publicly in the channel. Each entry shows the player and their count
out of the total that exist. Only current server members are listed; players who
left the server are excluded (their achievements are kept and they reappear if they
return).

Ranked by total achievements earned (see the full list on the
[Achievements](/platform/achievements) page). Achievements are awarded
when player cards are viewed, so the leaderboard reflects what's been earned and
recorded so far. See any player's individual collection with `/player-card`.
