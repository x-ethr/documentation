---
title: "`/demerit`"
---

Track and review demerits on player records.

## Subcommands

### `/demerit give`

Give a demerit to a user.

- `user` `User`
- `reason` `String`

The bot stores the demerit in the player record and also sends a DM to the user.

### `/demerit list`

List all demerits for a user.

- `user` `User`
