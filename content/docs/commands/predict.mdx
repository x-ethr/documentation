---
title: "`/predict`"
---

Predict the outcome of a matchup between two teams using the league's rating system.

## Options

- `team_a` `Role`
- `team_b` `Role`

## Output

- Win probability for each team, from the same expected-score curve the elo system uses
  (see [Mathematics](/platform/ratings)).
- What's at stake: the exact rating gain/loss for every scoreline (3-0, 3-1, 3-2) in
  either direction.

Both teams must have an established rating (teams still showing `~` cannot be
predicted).

## Crowns predictions

`/predict` is the odds calculator — it never stakes anything. Actually backing a
side with **Crowns**, the league's prediction currency, happens on the fixture's
page on [heir.gg](https://heir.gg), and those predictions are **public**: every
fixture page lists who backed which side, with stakes and locked odds while
predictions are open and actual payouts (winners first) after the match settles,
paginated for big leagues. In Discord, `/crowns balance` shows your balance and
open predictions and `/crowns leaderboard` the league's top 10.
