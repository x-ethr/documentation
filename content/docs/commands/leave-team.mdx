---
title: "`/leave-team`"
---

Leave your current team.

## Confirmation

The command replies with an ephemeral summary and a red **confirm** button
before doing anything — dismissing the message cancels. The summary spells out
the consequences for *your* situation:

- If you're the **captain**, captaincy passes to a random remaining member.
- If you're the **last member**, the team disbands.

## What Happens

- Your team role is removed
- Your `Free Agent` role is added
- Your player record is detached from the team
- If you were captain, a random remaining member becomes captain
- If you were the last player, the team is disbanded automatically
