---
title: "`/reform-team`"
---

Reform a previously disbanded team.

## Options

- `team` `Role`
- `captain` `User`
- `color` `String` optional

## Notes

- The archived team role must still exist
- The selected captain must not already be on a team
- The captain receives both the team role and the `Captain` role

<Callout type="warning">
If the archived team role was deleted manually, the bot cannot reform that team.
</Callout>
