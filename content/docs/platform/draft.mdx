---
title: "Draft Tool"
description: "Visual pug drafts — load the voice channel, crown captains, pick teams on screen."
---

Draft night without the spreadsheet: open **your league → Draft** and pick
teams visually, with every player's Heir Rating in view. Any league member can
run a draft — nothing here writes league data, so a draft is just a shared
screen and a copy-paste at the end.

<Callout type="info">
The draft tool is included with <strong>Pro</strong> and above.
</Callout>

## Building the pool

Three ways to fill the board:

- **From voice** — pick one or more voice channels (the list shows live
  occupancy) and everyone in them becomes the pool. Heir reads this straight
  from your Discord server.
- **From the whole roster** — skip voice entirely and draft from every league
  player, sorted by rating.
- Leagues without a linked Discord server go straight to the roster pool.

The **Refresh** button re-reads the voice channels without disturbing the
draft: players who left get a *left VC* badge (they stay pickable — they
probably just hopped to a team channel), new arrivals join the pool, and
nothing already drafted moves.

## Captains and pick order

Crown two captains with the **A / B** buttons on any player card — or drag a
player onto a team column. Captains are visually distinct (gold row, crown,
captain badge) and each team takes its captain's name.

Before starting you choose:

- **Alternate** (A, B, A, B…) or **Snake** (A, B, B, A…) pick order.
- **Who picks first** — hit the coin-flip chip and let fate decide.

## Drafting

The banner always says whose pick it is and how far along you are. Picking is
one click (**Pick**) or a drag onto the on-turn team — the other column dims
so a mis-drop can't happen. **Undo** takes back the last pick; **Setup** walks
the whole draft back if the captains want a redo (it asks first).

Every card shows the player's rating in their tier color, hovering shows the
usual mini profile, and unrated or unlinked players are still draftable from
their Discord name. Each team header keeps a running **average rating**, so
lopsided drafts are visible immediately.

## Present mode

The **Present** button opens a fullscreen stage made for screen sharing —
oversized turn banner, glowing on-turn team, and a gold flash as each pick
lands. Run the draft from there while everyone watches along in the call.
`Esc` exits.

## When it's done

**Copy teams** puts both rosters (captains first, ratings included) on your
clipboard, formatted for pasting straight into Discord. The board itself is
never saved — the pasted message is the record, exactly like a real draft
board gets erased after the game.
