---
title: "Web Profiles"
---

Every player and team in an activated league gets a public profile on
[heir.gg](https://heir.gg) — no login, no setup, nothing for organizers to
publish.

## What's on them

**Player profiles** show the Heir Rating with its skill breakdown, per-mode
ratings, career stat totals, recent matches, rating history, achievement badges
with guild-wide rarity, and full team history. Players who hold the Organizer
role wear an **Organizer** badge, and each linked game gets its own
**"&lt;Game&gt; Stats"** section with totals, a top-maps breakdown, and match
history.

**Team pages** show elo and record, the current roster with each player's rating,
elo history, match results, and season placements. A team's two-color gradient
carries through its name, chips, and page accents.

**League pages** show standings per region and a searchable, sortable directory
of every player, plus the tournaments, schedule, and leaderboards for the league.

## Finding your profile

Profile links are handed out by the bot and the web rather than searched for:

- `/player-card` links to your profile
- `/roster view` links to the team page
- Match rows on a profile link to the full match breakdown
- On the web, every name links to its profile

Links follow the shape `heir.gg/<league>/<player or team id>`, so they're stable
and shareable. Pasting one in Discord unfurls a card with the player's avatar,
rating, and tier.

## How current is it

Ratings and stat totals are recomputed by the bot the moment approved stats
change — a tracked match finishing, an organizer approving or voiding one, an
import, or a reclassification. Pages then cache briefly, so a profile is only ever
a short window behind the source of truth.

<Callout type="info">
Players who haven't run [`/identity link`](/commands/identity) still get a
profile — it just won't have in-game stats on it until they link.
</Callout>

## Privacy

Profiles are public but unlisted: the site has no player directory on its home
page, no name search, and asks search engines not to index it. A profile is
reachable only if someone has the link.
