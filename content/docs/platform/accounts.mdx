title: "Accounts & Linked Games"
---

Signing in to [heir.gg](https://heir.gg) with Discord claims the identity your
leagues already know — your Discord account **is** your Heir account. No
registration, no passwords, and Heir never asks for your email or server list.

## Your dashboard

Signed in, the home page becomes your competitive dashboard: your next
scheduled matches, your leagues, ratings, and teams. League owners and
Organizers also see **Manage** shortcuts on their leagues.

## What you can do on the web

The web app adapts to your role in each league:

| Role | On the web |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything below, plus `heir.gg/<league>/manage` — settings, channels, seasons, billing — and promoting Organizers |
| **Organizer** | The full manage surface (except owner-only bits), the schedule editor, tournaments, and stat approvals |
| **Captain** | Their team page — roster moves, availability grid, vacation, locations, and tournament sign-ups |
| **Player** | Their own profile and settings, plus read access to every public league page |

Most management that used to require Discord commands can now be done entirely
from the browser; for Discord-linked leagues the web hands your changes to the
bot to apply.

## Linked game accounts

Game accounts (like your Mordhau PlayFab ID) power automatic stat tracking —
they're how Heir knows which in-game player is you.

Link once, in either place:

- **Web** — [Settings → Game accounts](https://heir.gg/settings)
- **Discord** — `/identity link` in any of your leagues

Your link is stored on your **account** and Heir syncs it into every league
you play in, current and future — join a new league and your accounts are
already connected. Unlinking removes it everywhere the same way.

<Callout type="info">
<strong>PlayFab IDs are per-game.</strong> The same person has a different
PlayFab ID in every PlayFab-backed game, so each game on Heir asks for its own
ID. Platform IDs like your SteamID64 are the same everywhere.
</Callout>

## One account, one player

A game account can only be linked to **one** person per league. If your ID is
already claimed — an old smurf, a typo by someone else — contact one of your
league's Organizers, who can unlink it with `/identity admin-unlink`.
