---
title: "Running a tournament"
---

Everything happens on the tournament's page at [heir.gg](https://heir.gg) →
**Tournaments**. A tournament moves through four states: **draft** (building the
field), **group**/**bracket** (running), and **complete**. Only Organizers create
and run tournaments; captains and members interact through registration and, for
unranked cups, self-serve sign-ups.

## 1. Create

**Tournaments → New tournament.** Set:

- **Name**.
- **Format** — single or double elimination, or groups → elimination; bracket
  **size** (4/8/16/32, or a custom size on Pro); optional **third-place match**
  (single elim) and **grand-final reset** (double elim). Group stages set a group
  **count** and how many **qualify per group** — the two must multiply to the
  bracket size (e.g. 4 groups × 2 qualify = an 8-team bracket).
- **Ranked** — on to feed league elo (league teams only), off for a standalone or
  custom-team cup.
- **Registration** (optional) — open sign-ups, an optional **cap**, a **close
  time**, and whether members may **enter their own team** (unranked only).

A draft can be edited or deleted freely; nothing is announced until you start.

## 2. Build the field

Add teams while the tournament is a draft:

- **Add a league team** — pick from the league's active teams. A team already
  rostered in another live tournament match can't be double-booked.
- **Auto-fill** — one click fills the remaining slots with eligible league teams,
  ordered by elo.
- **Clear teams** — empties the field to start over.
- **Registration** — when open, captains enter their own team from the team page,
  and (if enabled) members enter a **custom team** built from existing league
  players. Entering a web-native league's tournament automatically makes you a
  player of that league.

### The field

During sign-ups the tournament page shows every registered team as a
team-colored tile with its entry number (or seed), roster size for self-entered
teams, and the registration date. Remaining capacity renders as open slots, so
anyone can see how much room is left. Your own entry is highlighted, with the
**Withdraw** button right on the tile.

### Seeding

Seeds decide the bracket layout. Drag teams to set seeds by hand; any teams you
leave unseeded are seeded by **elo** (strongest first) when the tournament starts.
Manual seeds always hold their position.

## 3. Start

**Start** locks the field, closes registration, seeds the bracket, and builds the
match graph. From here the format is fixed. A tournament needs at least 2 teams
(and group stages need at least 2 teams per group). Starting a groups format opens
the **group** stage first; a plain bracket opens straight into **bracket** play.

## 4. Play

The bracket renders on a pannable, zoomable **canvas** — drag to pan, scroll to
zoom, and use the minimap to navigate a large bracket. Group standings sit behind
a toggle on the same canvas.

- **Schedule a match** — click a match and set a start time. Scheduled matches DM
  both captains, send day-of and hour-before reminders, and open predictions.
- **Report a result** — click a **ready** match node (it shows a **Report**
  affordance) to open the score dialog, enter both scores, and record it. The
  winner advances into the next slot immediately, and in a group the standings
  recompute. Scores can't tie.
- **Void a result** — reopens a match. You have to void later matches that
  depended on it first (the bracket won't silently rewrite a played round).

### Recording matches in-game

On a tracked server, bind a bracket match to the collector with its 4-character
code — `.start K7QX` — exactly like a weekly fixture. The session is tracked and
its stats attach to that tournament match. For a **ranked** cup the series result
is recorded automatically (elo + league record) at the configured series length;
for **unranked**, the stats attach but the score is reported on the bracket. See
[Stat tracking](/platform/stat-tracking).

## 5. Finish

- **Finalize** — available once every match is played. It crowns the champion,
  archives final **placements** onto each team's profile, awards placement
  achievements, and posts the podium to Discord.
- **Cancel** — ends a running tournament with no champion. Played results stay on
  record; any open bets are refunded.

Ranked results move real elo, so they're applied by the bot; unranked results are
written directly from the web. Either way, the bracket and the podium are the same.
